# Flagship study thumbnails

These images are cropped, resized WebP derivatives of public-domain or
reuse-permitted archive scans.

- `tangerine-vol-1.webp`, `tangerine-vol-2.webp`, and
  `tangerine-vol-3.webp`: covers of *Secret in the Tangerine* volumes 1–3,
  National Archives of Japan item IDs
  [4756985](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/api/iiif/4756985/manifest.json),
  [4756986](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/api/iiif/4756986/manifest.json),
  and
  [4756987](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/api/iiif/4756987/manifest.json).
  Attribution: National Archives of Japan (国立公文書館所蔵). The archive's
  [secondary-use terms](https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/secondary-use)
  permit reproduction, modification, and redistribution.
- `golden-roc.webp`: title leaf of *Jin peng mi jue xiang qi ju*, Bavarian
  State Library shelfmark L.sin. L 59,
  [BSB digital copy](https://mdz-nbn-resolving.de/details:bsb00074184).
  Attribution: Bayerische Staatsbibliothek. The IIIF manifest marks the scan
  with the [Public Domain Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
- `seven-stars.webp`: cover of Charles Kliene's 1916 *Seven Stars: A Chinese
  Chess Ending with Three Hundred Variations*, digitized by Columbia University
  Libraries and hosted by the
  [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/ldpd_15172549_000). The 1916
  edition is in the public domain.
