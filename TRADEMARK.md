# Trademark Policy

This is a practical project policy, not a full legal trademark license.

The code is open source. The Mistboard name, logos, domains, hosted service identity, and official event names are controlled project assets.

For broader project direction and public-brand transition boundaries, see `docs/project-direction.md`.

## Allowed Uses

You may use the Mistboard name to:

- refer to the official project
- link to `mistboard.com`
- describe compatibility with Mistboard protocols or data formats
- state that your work is a fork of Mistboard
- discuss, review, criticize, or teach the project

## Not Allowed

You may not use the Mistboard name, logo, or confusingly similar branding to:

- imply your fork is the official Mistboard project
- impersonate `mistboard.com`
- run a competing hosted service under the Mistboard name
- publish packages or binaries that appear official without permission
- imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation without permission
- use Mistboard branding for scams, malware, cheating tools, or misleading services

## Forks

Forks are allowed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

Forks should use a distinct name and clearly state that they are not the official Mistboard project.

Acceptable:

> "MyProject is a fork of Mistboard."

Not acceptable:

> "Mistboard Pro" as an unofficial hosted service that users could confuse with `mistboard.com`.

## Events And Sponsorships

Official Mistboard tournaments, ladders, engine events, and sponsorship packages require maintainer approval.

Sponsors may receive approved logo placement or naming rights for a specific event, but sponsorship does not transfer ownership of the Mistboard name or brand.
