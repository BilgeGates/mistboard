# Roadmap

Mistboard is a platform for original strategy games, built for serious play:
server-enforced board games across chess, xiangqi, shogi, Jungle, and related
families, including hidden-information games such as dark chess.

Durable direction:

- Keep the hidden-information boundary correct and tested as variants land (see
  [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- Grow the public rules and [engine-protocol](engine-protocol.md) surface.
- Calibrate rated play before turning it on.

Milestones and tactical planning aren't tracked here. The queue is
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/brianhliou/mistboard/issues); direction and
contribution boundaries live in [project-direction.md](project-direction.md).
